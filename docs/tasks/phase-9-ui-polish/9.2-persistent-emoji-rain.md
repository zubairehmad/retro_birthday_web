# Task 9.2: Persistent Emoji Rain Lifecycle

## Objective
Refactor `EmojiRain` so celebratory particles continue falling smoothly without being destroyed or cut off mid-animation during screen transitions.

## Requirements
1. Move the `EmojiRain` component or state trigger up to `src/App.jsx` level so it is rendered independently of screen unmounting.
2. Ensure the particle cleanup timer executes fully (e.g., full 4–5 second trajectory) even after navigating from `InitialScreen` to `HbdScreen`.
3. Support non-blocking pointer events (`pointer-events: none`) on the rain overlay so users can interact with the target screen immediately while emojis fall.

## Constraints
- Avoid infinite re-renders or uncleared interval timers on unmount.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Emojis complete their full downward trajectory without popping out of existence during screen transitions.
- [ ] Rain overlay does not block button clicks on the newly revealed screen.
