# Patch Task 3.14: Remove HBD Song Smoothing

## Reason for Change

The HBD song should act as persistent background music for the HBD experience and continue underneath HBD sound effects. The previous fade lifecycle is unnecessary and can delay or prevent reliable stopping during navigation. HBD music must start and stop directly, without smoothing.

## Updated Requirements

1. Play `public/assets/audio/songs/happy_birthday.mp3` while HBD is mounted, with looping enabled.
2. Start the song directly at its configured playback volume; remove fade-in, fade-out, animation-frame, and delayed navigation behavior.
3. Keep the background song playing when HBD cake or other sound effects are triggered; effect playback must use independent audio instances.
4. Stop, reset, and release the HBD song immediately when HBD unmounts, including Continue navigation to Main Screen.
5. Prevent stale playback promises or callbacks from restarting or mutating the song after cleanup.
6. Keep the song scoped to HBD only; entering Main, Album, Letter, or Music screens must not retain HBD audio.
7. Preserve autoplay-safe error handling, HBD rendering, cake interaction, emoji rain, quote rotation, responsive behavior, and unrelated screen audio.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: `src/screens/HbdScreen.jsx` and related documentation only.
- Remove only the HBD background-song smoothing and navigation delay; retain guarded audio construction/playback and cleanup.
- Do not alter the song asset, audio effect utility, cake effects, emoji rain, navigation destinations, or Music Page controls.
- Do not edit completed 3.11, 3.12, or 3.13 task files.
- Do not add external dependencies, timers, animation frames, or global audio state.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Entering HBD starts one looping `happy_birthday.mp3` player at its configured volume without a fade-in.
- Cake and other HBD sound effects play without stopping or replacing the background song.
- Leaving HBD through Continue immediately pauses, resets, and releases the song without a fade-out delay.
- HBD audio does not continue on Main Screen or any other non-HBD screen.
- Autoplay rejection or unsupported playback is handled without unhandled errors or blocked navigation.
- Cleanup remains safe on rapid navigation, unmount during playback startup, and repeated HBD entry.
- Existing HBD layout, cake rain, quote rotation, navigation, and unrelated audio behavior remain intact.

**2026-09-04** — Replaced HBD song fade requirements with direct playback and immediate cleanup, while explicitly preserving background music during HBD sound effects. Specification-only update; no application code changes are included.
