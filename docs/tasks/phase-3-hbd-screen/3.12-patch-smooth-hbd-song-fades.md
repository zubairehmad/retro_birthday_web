# Patch Task 3.12: Add Smooth HBD Song Fade In and Fade Out

## Reason for Change

The HBD background song currently starts and stops abruptly. It should enter gently from silence and disappear smoothly when the user continues to the next screen, while remaining scoped to HBD and handling unmount, autoplay, and repeated navigation cleanup safely.

## Updated Requirements

1. Start `public/assets/audio/songs/happy_birthday.mp3` at `volume = 0` when HBD becomes active.
2. Fade the volume smoothly from 0% to 100% over a short, bounded interval while the song begins playing.
3. Keep the song looping at full volume for as long as HBD remains active.
4. When leaving HBD, cancel any active fade-in and smoothly reduce the current volume to 0% over a short, bounded interval before pausing and releasing the player.
5. Ensure cleanup is safe when unmount occurs during fade-in, fade-out, autoplay rejection, unsupported audio, rapid navigation, or repeated HBD entry.
6. Prevent duplicate audio instances, overlapping fade timers/animation frames, stale callbacks, and playback continuing after HBD unmounts.
7. Preserve HBD-only scope, browser autoplay-safe handling, cake sound effects, emoji rain, quote rotation, responsive layout, navigation, and Music Page playback.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: `src/screens/HbdScreen.jsx`, `src/utils/audio.js` or a narrowly scoped fade helper if required, and related documentation only.
- Reuse the existing bundled song path and explicit audio error-handling conventions.
- Do not alter the song asset, navigation model, emoji-rain implementation, cake interaction semantics, or unrelated screen audio.
- Do not edit completed 3.10 or 3.11 task files.
- Do not add external dependencies or leave timers, animation frames, event listeners, or audio objects alive after cleanup.

## Acceptance Criteria

- HBD song playback begins at 0% volume and audibly ramps smoothly to 100%.
- The song continues looping at full volume while HBD remains active.
- Leaving HBD starts a smooth fade from the current volume to 0%, then pauses and releases the audio player.
- No abrupt audible start/stop occurs during normal HBD entry or navigation away.
- Cleanup handles interruption at every fade stage without stale callbacks, duplicate players, unhandled promise rejections, or continued playback.
- Autoplay rejection or unsupported playback does not block HBD rendering, interaction, or navigation.
- The song remains silent and inactive on all non-HBD screens, and existing cake/audio, rain, quote, and navigation behavior remains intact.
