# Patch Task 3.10: Increase Emoji Rain Density and Stagger Timing

## Reason for Change

The current emoji rain still feels too sparse, especially after activating the HBD cake. Particles also enter too close together in time, making the effect look like a synchronized burst instead of a sustained, immersive rain. The celebration should become denser and use much broader timing variation so overlapping particles briefly fill nearly the whole viewport.

## Updated Requirements

1. Increase the bounded number of particles rendered for emoji-rain celebrations on both the Initial Screen and HBD Screen.
2. Give HBD cake-triggered rain a higher bounded density target than the Initial Screen celebration while preserving responsive performance.
3. Distribute particle spawn delays across a substantially wider bounded window so particles do not begin falling simultaneously.
4. Preserve varied entry positions, trajectory durations, drift, rotation, scale, opacity, and depth cues.
5. Ensure the staggered trajectories create a visible overlap period where nearly the whole viewport contains emojis, without permanently obscuring text or controls.
6. Preserve the existing approximately 1.5–2.5 second per-particle trajectory target, cleanup lifecycle, pointer transparency, repeatable cake activation, and Initial-to-HBD celebration continuity.
7. Keep particle generation bounded and avoid unbounded timers, DOM growth, continuous rendering, or external animation dependencies.
8. Preserve sad/mobile feedback behavior and all unrelated navigation, audio, quote, and responsive interactions.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: `src/components/EmojiRain.jsx`, `src/screens/InitialScreen.jsx`, `src/screens/HbdScreen.jsx`, and related `src/App.css` styles.
- Update the existing shared rain configuration or add a narrowly scoped screen-density option; do not duplicate the particle system.
- Do not alter emoji content, navigation destinations, audio utility behavior, cake interaction semantics, or unrelated screens.
- Do not edit completed 2.10, 2.11, 3.7, 3.8, or 3.9 task files.
- Do not add external dependencies or remove performance and overflow safeguards.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Initial-screen YES rain visibly contains more emojis than the current implementation and remains responsive.
- HBD cake-triggered rain is visibly denser than Initial-screen rain and produces an especially full celebration.
- Particles enter at clearly different times rather than appearing as one synchronized wave.
- During each celebration, staggered trajectories overlap enough that nearly the whole viewport contains emojis at a visible point in the burst.
- Particle counts, delay windows, trajectory durations, cleanup, and animation work remain explicitly bounded.
- Rain remains `pointer-events: none`, does not cause overflow, and does not prevent interaction with HBD content or controls.
- Repeated cake activations still produce complete, independent-looking bursts without stale particles or accumulating timers.
- Initial-to-HBD transition continuity, sad/mobile rain feedback, audio, navigation, quote rotation, and responsive layout remain intact.
