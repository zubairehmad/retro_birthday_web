# Patch Task 3.15: Persist HBD Song Across Screens

## Reason for Change

The HBD song is currently owned by `HbdScreen`, so it is destroyed when navigating to Main Screen. The song should begin when HBD is reached and continue across non-audio screens, while yielding playback when another song or video starts.

## Updated Requirements

1. Start one looping `public/assets/audio/songs/happy_birthday.mp3` player when HBD is first reached.
2. Keep that player active across Main Screen, Album, and Letter navigation.
3. Continue HBD music beneath HBD sound effects without pausing or replacing it.
4. Stop, reset, and release HBD music immediately before a playlist song, video, or other competing media source begins playback.
5. Release the player on application unmount and prevent duplicate players when revisiting HBD or changing screens.
6. Remove HBD-screen-unmount ownership assumptions; leaving HBD alone must not stop the persistent track.
7. Preserve direct full-volume playback without fade, autoplay-safe handling, HBD rendering, cake effects, emoji rain, quote rotation, navigation, and Music Page controls.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: `src/App.jsx`, `src/screens/HbdScreen.jsx`, `src/screens/MusicPage.jsx`, and related audio documentation only.
- Keep a single app-owned HBD player and provide a narrow handoff callback to competing audio.
- Do not alter the song asset, cake effects, emoji rain, quote rotation, navigation destinations, or unrelated audio behavior.
- Do not edit completed 3.11–3.14 task files.
- Do not add external dependencies, fades, timers, or duplicate global audio players.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Reaching HBD starts one looping birthday song at the configured volume.
- Navigating to Main, Album, or Letter leaves the birthday song playing.
- HBD cake/pop effects play without interrupting the birthday song.
- Starting a playlist track or other competing media pauses and releases the birthday song before playback begins.
- Revisiting HBD does not create overlapping birthday-song instances.
- Application unmount releases the birthday player safely.
- Autoplay or unsupported playback failures do not block rendering, navigation, or controls.

## Revision

**2026-09-04** — Added persistent app-scoped HBD playback, competing-media handoff, immediate ownership release, and no-fade requirements after the song stopped when Main Screen was opened. Specification-only update; no application code changes are included.
