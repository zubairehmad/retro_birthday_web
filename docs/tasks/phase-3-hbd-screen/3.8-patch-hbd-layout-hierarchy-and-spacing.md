# Patch Task 3.8: Fix HBD Layout Hierarchy and Spacing

## Reason for Change

Screenshot review shows the HBD screen rendering as a shallow full-width strip near the top of the viewport. The card lacks balanced outer whitespace, the title hierarchy is weak, and labels, message, cake control, quote, and Continue action are packed together. Decorative emojis also appear stranded near the viewport edge instead of forming an intentional composition.

## Updated Requirements

1. Make the HBD screen a centered composition with a constrained card width, balanced viewport whitespace, and a stable layout at desktop and mobile sizes.
2. Ensure the card has comfortable, consistent internal padding and vertical spacing between every major content section.
3. Establish one prominent, correctly styled birthday title followed by a concise congratulatory message and optional supporting sentence.
4. Separate the title, message, cake button, quote, and Continue action into visually clear sections with predictable alignment.
5. Position a small curated set of birthday emojis/decorations around the card without clipping, edge-stranding, text collisions, or control overlap.
6. Keep the birthday cake button obvious, accessible, and visually connected to its repeatable emoji-rain interaction.
7. Preserve quote rotation, cake sound, cake-triggered emoji rain, Continue navigation, responsive behavior, and no horizontal overflow.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: HBD layout and style surfaces identified during implementation, expected to include `src/screens/HbdScreen.jsx` and `src/App.css`.
- Do not alter navigation state, quote data, `EmojiRain` behavior, audio utility behavior, unrelated screens, or user-authored sound changes.
- Do not add external UI, layout, animation, or state-management dependencies.
- Do not edit completed HBD task files; this is a follow-up patch to completed task 3.7.

## Acceptance Criteria

- At desktop widths, the HBD card is visibly centered with substantial balanced whitespace around it and a readable maximum width.
- At mobile widths, the card remains contained, padded, and free of horizontal overflow.
- The birthday title is visually dominant, with properly spaced congratulatory and supporting text beneath it.
- Cake, quote, and Continue sections are clearly separated and do not collide or appear cramped.
- Decorations and emojis are intentional, fully visible, and do not interfere with content or controls.
- Cake activation still produces the visible emoji-rain celebration, and existing quote, audio, and navigation behavior remains intact.
