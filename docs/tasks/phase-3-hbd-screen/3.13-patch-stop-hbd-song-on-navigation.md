# Patch Task 3.13: Stop HBD Song Smoothly on Main Navigation

## Reason for Change

The HBD song can continue playing after the user navigates to Main Screen. The HBD audio lifecycle must reliably finish its smooth fade-out and release the player when the Continue navigation unmounts HBD.

## Updated Requirements

1. Ensure the HBD song begins the fade-out lifecycle whenever the user activates Continue and HBD is leaving for Main Screen.
2. Fade from the current volume to 0% before pausing, resetting, and releasing the HBD audio player.
3. Ensure the navigation transition does not leave the song playing after HBD unmounts, even if playback is still starting or fade-in is in progress.
4. Cancel or invalidate pending animation frames, promises, and callbacks so they cannot restart playback or modify audio after cleanup.
5. Prevent duplicate HBD players or stale audio state when entering HBD again.
6. Preserve HBD-only scoping, looping while active, autoplay-safe handling, cake effects, emoji rain, quote rotation, and all Main Screen behavior.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: `src/screens/HbdScreen.jsx`, `src/App.jsx` only if navigation coordination requires it, and related documentation.
- Reuse the existing HBD audio lifecycle and fade duration rather than introducing a second player or global background-audio system.
- Do not alter the song asset, Main Screen content, navigation destinations, cake interaction, emoji rain, or unrelated audio.
- Do not edit completed 3.11 or 3.12 task files.
- Do not add external dependencies or leave animation frames, timers, promises, listeners, or audio objects active after HBD cleanup.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Clicking Continue from HBD causes `happy_birthday.mp3` to fade smoothly to silence and stop before or during the HBD unmount lifecycle without audible continuation on Main Screen.
- Leaving HBD during fade-in, playback startup, or an autoplay rejection still results in safe cleanup with no stale callback restarting the song.
- Re-entering HBD creates at most one fresh active player and does not overlap a previous player.
- The song remains looped and HBD-scoped while HBD is active.
- Main Screen and all other non-HBD screens remain free of HBD background music.
- Existing HBD rendering, cake audio/rain, quote rotation, responsive layout, and navigation remain intact.
