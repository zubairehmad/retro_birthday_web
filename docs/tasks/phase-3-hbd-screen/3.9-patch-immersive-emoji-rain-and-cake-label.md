# Patch Task 3.9: Improve Emoji Rain and Cake Label Spacing

## Reason for Change

Emoji rain currently feels less immersive on both the Initial Screen and HBD Screen. The HBD cake button's hint text also sits too close to the control border and cake emoji, making the button look cramped.

## Updated Requirements

1. Improve emoji rain on both screens so particles span the viewport with varied, natural trajectories, stagger, drift, scale, opacity, and depth cues.
2. Preserve bounded particle counts, performance-safe cleanup, pointer transparency, and the existing 1.5–2.5 second trajectory target.
3. Ensure YES celebration rain and cake-triggered HBD rain meet the same immersive visual quality bar without obscuring text or controls.
4. Add consistent internal padding and line spacing to the HBD cake button so its hint label is separated from the cake emoji and button border.
5. Preserve accessible focus/pressed states, repeatable cake activation, quote rotation, audio behavior, navigation, responsive layout, and no-overflow constraints.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: `src/components/EmojiRain.jsx`, related rain styles in `src/App.css`, and HBD cake-button styles in `src/App.css`.
- Do not alter navigation, emoji data, audio utility behavior, screen content, or unrelated interactions.
- Do not add external animation or state-management dependencies.
- Do not edit completed 2.11, 3.7, or 3.8 task files.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Initial-screen YES rain visibly fills the viewport with varied, immersive trajectories while remaining performant and non-blocking.
- HBD cake-triggered rain has the same varied, immersive quality and does not obscure the cake, quote, Continue action, or other controls.
- Particle scale, opacity, drift, timing, and rotation variation remain bounded and cleaned up after each burst.
- The HBD cake button has balanced padding around both the emoji and hint label, with readable line spacing and visible separation from its border.
- Existing cake interaction, sound, quote rotation, navigation, responsive behavior, and pointer transparency remain intact.
