# Patch Task 3.16: Add HBD Fireworks Celebration

## Reason for Change

The HBD cake interaction currently produces emoji rain but lacks a stronger visual celebration. Each cake activation should launch a small group of realistic fireworks from the bottom of the viewport, with curved ascent, varied card-height bursts, complete animations, and distinct launch and pop sounds.

## Updated Requirements

1. On every cake click/tap, launch a bounded group of 4–5 fireworks from the bottom of the viewport.
2. Give each firework an independent start position, curved horizontal trajectory, duration, and pop height near the HBD card.
3. Animate each firework through launch, ascent, and visible pop/explosion stages using CSS or lightweight browser animation.
4. Keep every firework mounted until its own full ascent and pop sequence completes, including when later cake clicks occur.
5. Play one distinct launch sound and one distinct pop sound per firework or per clearly defined burst stage, using supported effect assets and safe playback handling.
6. Keep firework effects independent from and layered alongside the persistent HBD song; cake clicks must not render or restart emoji rain.
7. Make each pop substantially visible, using a larger radial explosion with multiple colored circles.
8. Make fireworks rise high: most bursts should land 100–400px from the top of the viewport, with one burst near the vertical midpoint.
9. Bound active fireworks, timers, DOM nodes, and animation work; clean up completed particles and all listeners/timers on unmount.
10. Preserve cake repeatability, HBD layout, background song persistence, quote rotation, navigation, responsive behavior, and existing audio handoff.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: `src/screens/HbdScreen.jsx`, a reusable firework component under `src/components/`, related styles in `src/App.css`, and firework sound assets under `public/assets/audio/effects/`.
- Reuse `playSound()` for launch and pop effects and keep effect sources separate from the looping birthday song.
- Do not alter the persistent HBD song ownership, initial-screen emoji-rain behavior, cake button semantics, navigation destinations, or unrelated screens.
- Do not edit completed 3.10–3.15 task files.
- Do not add external animation dependencies or unbounded particle generation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A cake activation visibly launches 4–5 fireworks from the viewport bottom.
- Fireworks rise along varied curved paths and pop at varied heights near the HBD card.
- Fireworks remain visible through their complete ascent and pop animation.
- Clicking the cake again while fireworks are active adds a new bounded burst without cancelling existing fireworks.
- Launch and pop use separate supported sound effects, and neither interrupts HBD background music.
- Completed fireworks and their timers/listeners are cleaned up; no stale fireworks remain after HBD unmount.
- Cake clicks show fireworks without emoji rain; initial-screen emoji rain, background-song persistence, audio handoff, navigation, quote rotation, and responsive layout remain intact.
