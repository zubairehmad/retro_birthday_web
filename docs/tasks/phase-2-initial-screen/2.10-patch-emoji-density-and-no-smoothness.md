# Patch Task 2.10: Increase Emoji Density and Smooth NO Evasion

## Reason for Change

The emoji rain still feels sparse, and the desktop NO button's escape behavior remains glitchy when the pointer approaches quickly or moves repeatedly across the card.

## Updated Requirements

1. Expand the configured happy emoji pool with additional celebration symbols while retaining the existing emojis.
2. Increase the visible particle density for happy and sad bursts within a bounded performance-safe limit.
3. Keep emoji trajectories brisk, completing in approximately 1.5–2.5 seconds with short staggered delays.
4. Refine desktop NO-button evasion to choose a position away from the pointer, avoid repeated identical targets, and coalesce rapid pointer updates to prevent jitter.
5. Preserve strict parent-container bounds, fast smooth transforms, and no horizontal overflow.
6. Keep desktop cursor evasion disabled for coarse/touch pointers while retaining mobile NO audio and sad emoji feedback.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: `src/data/birthdayData.js`, `src/components/EmojiRain.jsx`, `src/screens/InitialScreen.jsx`, and related `src/App.css` styles.
- Do not alter navigation destinations, audio utility error handling, root-level celebration ownership, or unrelated screens.
- Do not add external dependencies or unbounded particle generation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Happy emoji rain visibly contains more varied and abundant symbols than the current burst.
- Happy and sad particles remain capped and complete their fall within the brisk timing target.
- Rapid desktop pointer movement causes smooth, directional NO-button escapes without visible jitter or repeated no-op jumps.
- The NO button never leaves its interaction container or introduces page scrollbars.
- Mobile/touch NO interaction continues to play its sound and show sad emoji rain without desktop evasion logic.
