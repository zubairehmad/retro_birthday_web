# Task 6.2: Style Vintage Stationary & Handwritten Typography

## Objective
Apply realistic aged paper aesthetics and vintage typography to the letter[cite: 1, 2].

## Requirements
1. Style letter paper with warm cream background texture, subtle drop shadow, and folded/torn paper edges[cite: 1, 2].
2. Apply cursive/handwritten font family to letter body text with readable line spacing[cite: 1, 2].
3. Add decorative wax seal or tape graphic element at header/footer[cite: 1, 2].

## Constraints
- Ensure cursive font remains fully legible across all screen resolutions[cite: 1].

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Stationary visually resembles an authentic vintage letter or open card[cite: 1, 2].
- [ ] Text remains highly legible on mobile and desktop viewports[cite: 1].
