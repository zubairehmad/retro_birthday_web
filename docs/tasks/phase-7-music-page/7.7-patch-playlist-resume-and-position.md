# Patch Task 7.7: Resume HBD Music and Preserve Playlist Position

## Reason for Change

Opening playlist playback correctly pauses the HBD background song, but the background music is discarded instead of being resumable. The playlist effect also reloads the selected track whenever `isPlaying` changes, resetting its position and duration.

## Updated Requirements

1. Pause, rather than destroy, HBD background music when playlist playback starts.
2. Resume HBD background music when playlist playback is paused, stopped, fails, or the playlist is exited.
3. Do not resume HBD music when switching directly from one playlist track to another while playback continues.
4. Keep the current playlist audio element, position, and duration when toggling play/pause.
5. Reset position and duration only when selecting a different track.

## Boundaries / Impact

- Affected files: `src/App.jsx` and `src/screens/MusicPage.jsx`.
- Preserve existing responsive Music layouts and record rotation.
- Do not change unrelated audio effects or screens.

## Acceptance Criteria

- HBD music pauses when a playlist track starts and resumes when playlist playback stops.
- Leaving the playlist resumes HBD music when it had been playing.
- Pausing and resuming a track retains its current time and loaded duration.
- Selecting a different track starts that track from its beginning.
- `npm run build` passes.
